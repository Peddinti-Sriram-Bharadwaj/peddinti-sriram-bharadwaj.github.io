A hundred billion thousand planets

27 September 2026first posted on Substack ↗

A hundred billion thousand planets in the universe..

and we..

are the only life as we know so far.

And some of us are trying to build an Artifical Intelligence..

How fascinating of a time in the cosmic scale of events are we living in…, soon is the day the universe truly comes alive, moving beyond the mere replicating group of molecules to omnipotence.

These are the thoughts running in my mind as I made up my mind towards an academic future, embarking on my journey towards understanding the internals of Artificial Intelligence.

In a universe that is vastly hollow, drizzled with stars and dust here and there, we..a tiny speck..are the ones with sentient life, and soon potentially artifical life. The identity crisis is real, we might just be an intermediary phase of carbonic beings that build the eternal life that can take up any form body and manufacture its embodiment. Perhaps this is the long running mythological concept of reincarnation come true.

If I observe things just with with my biological eyes…I might be losing out on income and travel. But as I widen my perspective to a cosmic scale, I am but making a mere grand sacrifice, one that is a lot but is not depending on how narrow or wide of a cosmos you feel.

Trading my views of the physical world and madeup currency, in pursuit of eyes that enable me peer into the depths of the unfathomable Artificial Intelligence, One thing has become increasingly clear. Whatever unfulfilled materialistic wishes I had simply felt like trinkets on the grand cosmic scale, compared to the grand cosmic journey.

The parallels I see with fiction are immense. The way Rune King Thor emerges sacrificing his vision for a cosmic eye, the way the Sharingan evolves enabling the wielder peer deeper and deeper into the world, I am thus harnessing my brainpower towards a new sight.

This sight perceives mere vector calculus and infers knowledge, consciousness.

While the question of humanitys survival at the hands of superintelligence is to answered, one thing is for certain. We are here, at this moment, the grandest thing this cosmos has ever borne witness to.

Like us, there is more to AI than meets the eye. I am Sriram Bharadwaj, and this message to any fellow crewmates journeying on the pale blue dot. We are here, We are making history.

(Cue “What I’ve done” from Linking parks playing in the background )

With this note, I declare my humble beginnings of an undying researcher, observing the mysteries of life, consciousness and what it means to be an unartifical life in this vast existence.

~ Sriram
