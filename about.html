About

Peddinti Sri Ram Bharadwaj, though most people call me Sriram.

I am a reinforcement learning researcher. Right now I'm working independently on continual self-play for LLM post-training, and on plasticity collapse: why networks slowly lose the ability to learn when the world they train in keeps changing.

I came to this through an M.Tech in Computer Science at IIIT Bangalore and a research internship at IISc's Stochastic Systems Lab, where I co-developed the SOR family of Bellman update rules. I like working at both ends of the stack. On one end is measure-theoretic RL theory. On the other is the machinery underneath it: Vulkan compute shaders on Android, and the build internals of PyTorch and ExecuTorch.

Outside the lab I write by hand, and I've been reading Camus. The absurd keeps turning up in my essays.
